StarkVoice is a innovative Decentralized Proposal Voting Platform on Starknet. This platform leverages blockchain technology to ensure trust, transparency, and efficiency in voting on various proposals. This platform will primarily be available to Starknet.
StarkVoice supports following features:
- Time Bounded Voting
- Permissioned Voting
- Token Weighted Voting
